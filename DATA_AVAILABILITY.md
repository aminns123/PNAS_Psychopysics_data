# Proposed Data, Materials, and Software Availability wording

## Initial submission, if the authors commit to completing the full deposit

Deidentified psychophysical data, derived contrast-sensitivity and preferred-spatial-frequency results, the saved resampling distributions, and the analysis and modelling materials needed to reproduce the reported findings will be deposited in a permanent public repository and made available no later than publication. The materials will be provided to editors and reviewers during evaluation upon request.

This is proposed author wording, not a claim that the repository already exists. Before using it, resolve the release questions and ensure the final modelling and other figure materials will be included. The present prepared package covers the CSF/PSF data component and records the remaining reproducibility issues.

## After a complete, verified public release

Deidentified psychophysical data, derived contrast-sensitivity and preferred-spatial-frequency results, resampling distributions underlying the reported uncertainty estimates, and the analysis and modelling materials supporting this study are available at [repository name and version] ([DOI]).

Replace the bracketed text with a real permanent record, verify access and scope, and cite that record in the manuscript references. Do not paste placeholder DOI text into a submitted final statement.
